Realtime Overview

Opens a separate window with a live, fit-to-window view of the active document. Move it to another monitor and double-click for fullscreen.

Unlike the Overview docker, it keeps updating while the Transform tool (or any other long stroke) is active.

Docker: Settings > Dockers > Realtime Overview. Dock it, tab it, or float it onto another monitor (double-click the preview to float/dock).

Window: Tools > Scripts > Realtime Overview (assign a shortcut in Settings > Configure Krita > Keyboard Shortcuts).

Right-click the window for options: fullscreen, mirror, grayscale, checkerboard, sampling quality, refresh rate, always on top, focus behaviour.
